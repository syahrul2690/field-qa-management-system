## Overview & Scope

The Site Quality Plan (SQP) defines all quality control activities (inspections and tests) to be performed on-site for the contractual scope of supply during installation, commissioning, and performance test phases. It is a subsidiary plan of the Project Management Plan and serves as a critical communication tool among all involved stakeholders.

### Purpose and Application

- **Specification:** Planned inspections and tests to be performed on-site during plant erection, equipment installation, and commissioning phases
- **Responsibility Definition:** Indicates the party responsible for execution of each listed inspection and test
- **Role Clarity:** Defines roles of all parties involved in quality control processes on-site (Subcontractors, Contractor=Wärtsilä, Customer=Owner, Third Parties)
- **Timeline:** Created within four weeks from finalization of detailed design
- **Project-Specific:** SQP is always project-specific, varying based on Contract type and Project delivery method

### Quality Assurance and Control Integration

The Site Quality Plan is an essential component of Wärtsilä's Quality Assurance and Control process, operating in conjunction with the Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) to define quality assurance and quality control activities planned for:
- Equipment manufacturer factory testing
- Power Plant construction site activities
- Installation and commissioning phases
- Performance verification and acceptance testing

## Quality Control Involvement Roles and Definitions

### Four Possible Quality Control Roles

#### **E: EXECUTE** (Execution Responsibility)
- **Definition:** Party responsible for performing the inspection or test
- **Obligations:**
  - Send notification of test/inspection to all concerned parties
  - Perform the inspection or test as specified
  - Document all results and findings
  - Maintain test records and certificates

#### **W: WITNESS POINT** (Witness Authority)
- **Definition:** Party has optional authority to attend and observe test execution
- **Authority Level:** Non-mandatory attendance; test may proceed if witnessing party is absent
- **Waiver Option:** Witnessing party may provide written waiver of attendance
- **Documentation:** Witness shall verify test execution if present

#### **H: HOLD POINT** (Hold/Mandatory Witness Authority)
- **Definition:** Test or inspection cannot proceed without party's presence or written authorization
- **Authority Level:** Mandatory hold until witness is present
- **Waiver Process:** Witnessing party must provide written authorization to proceed if absent
- **Critical Gate:** Used for critical safety, performance, and acceptance testing
- **Examples:** Engine alignment, hold points, critical electrical connections, performance test hold points

#### **R: REVIEW** (Review and Approval Authority)
- **Definition:** Proof of test/inspection by review and approval of documentation
- **Scope:** Reviews test data, certificates, and quality records
- **Authority:** Reviews and approves completion of specified inspection or test
- **Documentation:** Maintains approval records and document control

#### **[Blank Field]:** NO ROLE
- No role in quality control process is assigned to this party

## Technical Glossary - Quality Control Terms

| Term | Definition | Application in SQP |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Inspection** | Visual examination of equipment, components, or systems for conformance to specifications | Installation inspections (I-series) |
| **Test** | Verification of equipment functionality, performance, and safety characteristics | Functionality tests (F-series) |
| **Hold Point** | Critical gate where work must stop until authorized party approval is obtained | **H** role designation |
| **Witness Point** | Optional attendance gate where party may observe but test may proceed if absent | **W** role designation |
| **Review Point** | Documentation review and approval of test results and certificates | **R** role designation |
| **Execution** | Performance of the test or inspection activity with full responsibility | **E** role designation |
| **Certificate** | Formal document verifying test completion and acceptance | C-series documents |
| **Installation Phase** | Period of equipment installation and preliminary verification | I-series tests |
| **Commissioning Phase** | Period of system startup, functional verification, and parameter optimization | F-series tests |
| **Performance Test** | Verification that equipment meets specified performance parameters under operating conditions | C-series tests |
