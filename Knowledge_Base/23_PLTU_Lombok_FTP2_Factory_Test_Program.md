## 2. Scope of Works (Factory & Field Testing)

### 2.1 Overall Project Phases
The Contractor is responsible for:
1. **Design** of all systems per contract technical requirements
2. **Manufacture** of boiler, turbine, generators, and balance of plant equipment
3. **Factory Testing** (FTP) - comprehensive shop tests per this FTP program
4. **Delivery & Logistics** to site in Lombok, Indonesia
5. **Installation** - assembly and integration of all plant systems
6. **Pre-Commissioning** - preparatory checks and initial functional tests
7. **Commissioning & Performance Testing** - full system startup and performance verification
8. **Warranty Period** - defect remediation for contractual warranty duration

### 2.2 Core Systems Subject to FTP (Factory Test Program)

#### 2.2.1 Boiler System (Coal-Fired CFB)
- Boiler metal pressure parts (drums, tubes, headers)
- Boiler casing and refractory
- Furnace integrity and air-tight design
- Thermal insulation systems
- Boiler instrumentation and control connections
- **Critical Hold Points:** Pressure vessel hydrostatic test, tube tightness test, refractory cure verification

#### 2.2.2 Turbine & Generator System
- Turbine rotor assembly and blade installation
- Generator rotor and stator manufacturing
- Coupling and alignment verification
- Bearing assembly and preload
- Control system integration for turbine governing
- **Critical Hold Points:** Rotor balance verification, bearing run-in tests, vibration acceptance criteria

#### 2.2.3 Balance of Plant (BOP) Equipment
- Condenser (tube bundle integrity and cleanliness)
- Cooling water circulation pump and motor
- Feedwater heater assembly and testing
- Condensate and hot well systems
- Ash handling and air separation equipment
- **Critical Hold Points:** Condenser tube bundle leak rate, pump cavitation and flow tests

#### 2.2.4 Electrical & Control Systems
- High Voltage (HV) generator switchgear
- Power transformers (unit transformer, auxiliary transformer)
- Medium Voltage (MV) switchboards
- Low Voltage (LV) panels and distribution
- SCADA/DCS system factory acceptance testing (FAT)
- Excitation and synchronization systems
- **Critical Hold Points:** Dielectric tests, control system functional tests, synchronization verification

#### 2.2.5 Auxiliary Systems
- Fuel handling and preparation
- Compressed air systems
- Fire detection and suppression
- HVAC and ventilation
- Waste water treatment
- Lube oil and hydraulic systems
- **Critical Hold Points:** System commissioning and performance baseline

## 3. Factory Test Program (FTP) Phases & Structure

### 3.1 Pre-Commissioning Phase (Factory)

#### 3.1.1 Document Preparation & Approval
- **Acceptance Criteria:** All drawings, specifications, test procedures approved by Owner before testing
- **Responsible Parties:** Contractor (prepares), Owner/PLN (approves), Independent Inspector (witnesses critical reviews)
- **Hold Point (H):** No shop testing shall commence until all design documents approved

#### 3.1.2 Pre-Fabrication Inspections
- Material receiving, mill certificates, dimensional conformance
- Welding WPS (Welding Procedure Specification) and PQR (Procedure Qualification Record) review
- Welder certification verification
- CNC/machining setup verification for critical components
- **Inspection Levels:** First Article Inspection (FAI), 100% material verification for pressure parts

#### 3.1.3 Manufacturing Inspections (In-Process)
- **Inspection Activity Codes:**
  - **R** = Review (Contractor prepares, Owner reviews documentation)
  - **W** = Witness (Owner/PLN attends and witnesses work in progress)
  - **H** = Hold Point (Mandatory Owner/PLN sign-off before proceeding)

### 3.2 Pressure Vessel & Piping Tests (Foundation for FTP)

#### 3.2.1 Hydrostatic Pressure Tests
Applied to all pressure-containing components: boiler drums, condenser, air heater, etc.

| Component | Test Pressure | Duration | Acceptance Criteria |
|-----------|---------------|----------|-------------------|
| Boiler Drum | **1.5 × Design Pressure** | 30 minutes hold | **Zero visible leakage; stress analysis verification** |
| Condenser Shell | **1.5 × Design Pressure** | 20 minutes | **Tube-to-tube sheet integrity maintained** |
| Heater Bundles | **1.5 × Design Pressure** | 15 minutes | **All tube joints leak-free** |
| Piping Spools | **1.5 × Design Pressure** (or per ASME B31.1) | Per spool length | **No permanent deformation** |

**Hold Point (H):** Hydrostatic test is mandatory **Hold Point**. No item shall proceed to next phase without Owner's written pressure test acceptance. Test report must include:
- Pressure gauge calibration certificates (±1% accuracy)
- Pressure-time chart (recorder trace or digital record)
- Inspector sign-off on visual inspection (zero weeping)
- Nonconformance corrective actions (if any)

#### 3.2.2 Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)
All welded pressure boundary joints subject to:
- **Radiographic Test (RT):** Butt-weld seams (100% per ASME Section V and B31.1)
- **Ultrasonic Test (UT):** Thick sections and austenitic stainless steel
- **Penetrant Test (PT):** Socket-weld and fillet-weld connections
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Per ASME Section VIII Div.1 (boiler vessels) and B31.1 (piping)

**Critical NDE Hold Points:**
- All Code-required welds must be examined prior to hydrostatic test
- Defects identified must be repaired and re-examined
- NDE acceptance is **Hold Point (H)**

#### 3.2.3 Cleanliness & Passivation (Stainless Steel Systems)
For systems with austenitic stainless steel components (feedwater, condensate):
- **Passivation per ASTM A967** for all stainless welds
- **Iron removal flush test** to verify contamination removal
- **Final surface inspection** for white precipitate or corrosion products
- Acceptance: Iron content per ASTM A967 limits

**Hold Point (H):** All austenitic systems must be passivated and flushfree before closure.

## 4. Mechanical Test Program (FTP Phases)

### 4.1 Turbine Rotor Manufacturing & Balancing

#### 4.1.1 Rotor Assembly
- Blade installation with stress-free assembly
- Coupling connection torque verification
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Blade root stress ≤ design limit per finite element analysis (FEA)

#### 4.1.2 Rotor Dynamic Balancing
- **Test Method:** Spin test at manufacturer's balancing machine
- **Test Speed:** Minimum 110% of Maximum Continuous Operating Speed (MCOS)
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - **Residual unbalance:** ≤ ISO G2.5 (per ISO 21940-11) for steam turbine rotors
  - **Vibration amplitude:** ≤ 100 μm at bearing housings during spin test
  - **Bearing temperatures:** Normal operating range (no overheating)

**Hold Point (H):** No rotor shall leave factory without certification of dynamic balance. Balance certificate must include:
- Balance plane locations and correction values
- Test bearing readings (amplitude & phase)
- Witness signature from Owner/Independent Inspector

### 4.2 Generator Stator & Rotor Assembly

#### 4.2.1 Stator Winding Insulation Tests
- **Megohm Test (Insulation Resistance):** Minimum 1 MΩ per kV rating at 20°C (e.g., 20 MV for 20 kV machine)
- **Hi-Pot Test:** Per manufacturer design (typically 2.5 × nominal for acceptance)
- **Partial Discharge (PD) Test:** If specified in contract
- **Acceptance Criteria:** No breakdown, no corona discharge detected

**Hold Point (H):** Insulation acceptance mandatory before rotor insertion.

#### 4.2.2 Rotor Winding Insulation
- Megohm test for rotor field winding
- Accepted resistance value per manufacturer (typically ≥ 10 MΩ)

#### 4.2.3 Air-Gap Inspection
- **Visual inspection** of stator bore and rotor OD for debris, foreign objects
- Rotor run-through at low speed to verify clearance
- **Hold Point (H):** Any foreign object must be removed before proceeding.

### 4.3 Bearing & Lubrication System Tests

#### 4.3.1 Lube Oil System Commissioning (Factory)
- Flushing of all lube oil piping and coolers
- **Iron content limit:** ≤ 50 ppm before equipment operation
- Filtration to ISO 18/16/13 cleanliness
- Oil analysis (particle count, viscosity, acid number per ISO 4406)

#### 4.3.2 Bearing Run-In Test
- **Test Duration:** 72 hours continuous operation at low load
- **Operating Conditions:** 
  - Rotor speed: 40–80% MCOS (gradual ramp)
  - Bearing temperature: Monitor continuously (alert at 70°C, shutdown at 80°C for water-cooled bearings)
  - Lube oil outlet temperature: < 55°C
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - No temperature rise trend
  - Stable bearing vibration amplitude (< 200 μm)
  - Oil pressure nominal (per design)
  
**Witness Point (W):** Owner attendance for start-up and critical load transitions.

### 4.4 Condenser Performance Test (Shop)

#### 4.4.1 Tube Bundle Integrity Test
- **Hydrostatic test:** Tube-side at 1.5 × design pressure
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Zero tube-to-tube sheet leakage
  - Zero tube-to-tube leakage between passes

#### 4.4.2 Cleanliness Inspection
- **Visual inspection** of all tubes via borescope (if required by contract)
- **Air blowing** to remove debris and dust from tubes
- **Acceptance:** Tubes visually clean, ready for operation

#### 4.4.3 Cooling Test (if condensing system available)
- Circulate cooling water; monitor temperature rise in condenser shell
- Verify condenser effectiveness coefficient (UA value) ≥ 95% of design
- **Acceptance Criteria:** UA per manufacturer's design specification

## 5. Electrical System FTP

### 5.1 Power Transformer Testing

#### 5.1.1 Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT) for Generator Transformer
**Mandatory Tests per IEC 60076:**

| Test | Criterion | Hold Point |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Insulation Resistance (Megohm)** | ≥ 100 MΩ at 20°C, HV winding to ground | H |
| **Dielectric Strength (Hi-Pot)** | 2.5 kV × (HV phase voltage/1 kV) for 1 min, no breakdown | H |
| **Winding Resistance** | Measured & recorded; tolerance ±2% from design | W |
| **Turns Ratio (TTR)** | Within ±0.5% from nameplate ratio per tap | R |
| **Inductance & Leakage Reactance** | Per design ± 10% | R |
| **Partial Discharge (PD)** | If specified: ≤ 5 pC at test voltage | H |
| **Oil Quality (if oil-cooled)** | Water content < 50 ppm, dielectric strength ≥ 30 kV, particle cleanliness ISO 18/16/13 | H |
| **Thermal Test** | No-load and loaded loss within design tolerance | W |

**Critical Hold Points:**
- Insulation resistance (Megohm test) **MUST** pass before hi-pot
- Hi-pot test is **Hold Point (H)**
- Oil quality acceptance is **Hold Point (H)**

#### 5.1.2 Power Transformer Shipping & Receiving Inspection
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - No dents, cracks, or oil leaks observed
  - Pressure relief valve operational and sealed
  - Gas accumulator (if equipped) pre-charged per design
  - **Verifying Document:** Third-party inspection certificate or Owner's receiving inspection report

### 5.2 Generator Control & Excitation System FAT

#### 5.2.1 Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) Functional Test
- **Test Setup:** Generator simulator or actual prime mover (reduced load)
- **Test Sequence:**
  1. Exciter field energization check
  2. Generator voltage ramp-up (no load): voltage rise from 0 to 110% rated in ≤ 5 seconds
  3. Voltage regulation measurement: ±2% at ±10% load step
  4. Response time to load disturbance: settling within 1–2 seconds
  5. Reverse power protection: Trigger alarm at < -10% power
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Voltage rise smooth, no oscillation
  - Final voltage steady-state ± 2% nominal
  - Protection functions verified and logged

**Hold Point (H):** Synchronization system acceptance mandatory before field synchronization test.

#### 5.2.2 Control System Functional Test (SCADA/DCS)
- **Test Scope:** Logic verification, interlock checking, operator interface functionality
- **Test Method:**
  - Simulated sensor inputs (signal generators)
  - Verification of permissive logic (e.g., fuel cannot feed unless air fan running)
  - Alarm triggering and notification
  - Data logging function check
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - All critical sequences operate per logic diagram
  - No unauthorized start permissives
  - Alarms trigger within specified time
  - Data recording verified for 24 hours

**Hold Point (H):** Logic interlocks that prevent unsafe operation must be verified.

### 5.3 Switchgear & Busbar Testing

#### 5.3.1 Generator Switchgear FAT (HV Breaker, Isolators, etc.)
- **Insulation resistance:** ≥ 10 MΩ per phase (dry/clean atmosphere)
- **Hi-Pot test:** 2.15 kV for 1 minute (for 6.3 kV or 11 kV equipment class)
- **Mechanical operation:** 
  - Trip time: ≤ 100 ms after fault signal
  - Close time: ≤ 200 ms from energization command
  - Repeatability: 10 cycles with no variance > 10%
- **Contact resistance:** Measured before and after mechanical tests; variance ≤ 5%
- **Verification Document:** Factory test report with witness signature

**Hold Point (H):** Trip time and insulation resistance are mandatory hold points.

#### 5.3.2 Bus Duct Acceptance
- **Assembly and alignment:** Visual inspection, no deformation
- **Insulation resistance:** ≥ 100 MΩ (all phases to ground)
- **Hi-Pot test:** 2.15 kV × 1 min for 6.3/11 kV duct
- **Contact pressure verification:** Spring pressure within design range
- **Forced air ventilation test:** Air flow rate ≥ design CFM
- **Acceptance Criteria:** All parameters within specification

## 6. Control & Instrumentation Testing

### 6.1 Distributed Control System (DCS) / Programmable Logic Controller (PLC)

#### 6.1.1 Software Verification
- **Source Code Review:** Logic diagrams vs. implemented code
- **Module Test:** Each control block tested individually with simulated inputs
- **Integration Test:** Multiple modules tested together (sequences, interlocks)
- **System Test:** Full system simulation with realistic plant data
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Code matches design specification; all test cases passed

#### 6.1.2 Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Testing
- **Simulated Plant Model:** Real-time simulation of boiler, turbine, generator dynamics
- **Controller Under Test:** Actual PLC/DCS connected to simulator
- **Test Scenarios:**
  - Normal startup sequence
  - Load change (10%, 25%, 50%, 100%)
  - Trip scenarios (turbine trip, fuel cutoff, etc.)
  - Recovery and restart
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Setpoints maintained within tolerance
  - Transient overshoot < design limit
  - Protection interlocks prevent unsafe operation

**Hold Point (H):** Control system must pass HIL test before field acceptance.

### 6.2 Instrumentation (Sensors, Transmitters, Indicators)

#### 6.2.1 Flow Measurement Instrumentation
- **Calibration:** Traceable to national standards (NPL, PTB, or equivalent)
- **Calibration Range:** 0–120% of maximum operating flow
- **Accuracy Acceptance:** ± 2% of full scale (or per specification)
- **Certificate:** Individual calibration certificate for each transmitter
- **Witness:** Owner representative may witness selected calibrations (Witness Point)

#### 6.2.2 Temperature Measurement (Thermocouples, RTDs)
- **Type Verification:** Material certification matching thermocouple type (K, J, etc.)
- **Calibration:** Ice point (0°C) and steam point (100°C) or reference bath
- **Acceptance Tolerance:** ± 1°C or ± 2% of range (whichever greater)
- **Protection Wells:** Installed correctly; no obstruction of measurement

#### 6.2.3 Pressure Transmitters
- **Range Verification:** 0–120% of design operating pressure range
- **Accuracy:** ± 0.5% FS (full scale) for critical services, ± 1% FS for non-critical
- **Calibration Equipment:** Deadweight tester or certified calibration system
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Zero, mid-range, and span points within tolerance

**Hold Point (H):** Critical instrumentation used for plant protection (overpressure, over-temperature) must be calibrated and certified before operation.

## 8. Shop Test Inspection Procedures (Section Schedule 1.7)

### 8.1 Contractor's Responsibility (KSO / Kontraktor)
- **Activity Code "W":** Contractor conducts work; prepares verification documents
- **Self-Inspection:** Contractor Quality Inspector witnesses and signs verification document
- **Records:** Test reports, inspection checklists, certificates maintained in QMS

### 8.2 Owner's Responsibility (PLN)
- **Activity Code "R":** Owner reviews documentation submitted by Contractor
- **Activity Code "W":** Owner witness (physical presence during test/inspection)
- **Activity Code "H":** Hold Point; Owner/PLN representative must sign off before work can proceed
- **Sign-Off Authority:** PLN Engineer, PLN Inspector, or Independent Inspector (per contract)

### 8.3 Inspection Report & Verifying Documents

#### 8.3.1 Standard Verifying Documents for FTP
- **Test Report:** For performance and functional tests (hydrostatic test, balance verification, FAT)
- **Inspection Report:** For visual and dimensional inspections
- **Certificate:** For calibration, certification of welders, material mill certificates
- **As-Built Drawing:** Updated drawing reflecting final configuration
- **Witness Sheet:** Signed by both Contractor and Owner representatives

#### 8.3.2 Nonconformance & Corrective Action
If a test fails or an inspection reveals nonconformance:
1. **NCR (Non-Conformance Report) Issued** by Owner/Inspector
2. **Root Cause Analysis** by Contractor
3. **Corrective Action Plan** submitted to Owner
4. **Rework & Retest:** Component corrected and re-tested
5. **Re-Inspection:** Owner verifies corrective action effective
6. **Closure:** NCR closed only after Owner's written acceptance

**Mandatory Documentation:** All NCR reports, corrective actions, and closure approvals retained for handover.

## 12. Key Lessons & Commissioning Readiness

### 12.1 Common FTP Issues & Resolution
1. **Incomplete Documentation:** Ensure all test procedures approved before work begins
2. **Pressure Test Delays:** Plan hydrostatic test window in advance; arrange water supply, pressure pump
3. **Insulation Failures:** Manage humidity during factory assembly; dry all electrical components before hi-pot
4. **Imbalance Rejection:** Validate balance machine calibration; plan rotor balancing as late-stage activity
5. **Acceptance Criteria Ambiguity:** Define acceptance values quantitatively (e.g., "±2%" not "acceptable")

### 12.2 Transition to Field Testing
- **Pre-Commissioning Readiness:** All FTP certificates collected and verified
- **Spare Parts Availability:** Mandatory spare parts (Schedule 1.4) delivered with equipment
- **Field ITP Preparation:** Owner/Contractor agree on Field ITP before commencement
- **Site Readiness:** Civil foundations, utilities (power, water, fuel) confirmed before equipment arrival
- **Training:** Contractor's training per Schedule 1.3 (typically 4–8 weeks on-site for plant operations & maintenance staff)
