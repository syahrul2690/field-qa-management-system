# Fire Fighting & Fire Alarm System — Inspection & Test Plan Knowledge Base

*Consolidated from Multiple ITP Documents*

## Overview & Scope

This knowledge base consolidates Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) procedures for Fire Fighting and Fire Alarm systems in power generation plant installations. The ITP encompasses both shop fabrication/assembly testing and field installation verification activities.

### Applicable Project Documents
- A-3.04.00006: Shop ITP Fire Fighting & Fire Alarm System (Status A)
- A-3.04.00013: Shop ITP Fire Fighting & Fire Alarm System (Status B)
- A-3.04.00014: Field ITP Fire Fighting & Fire Alarm System (Status B)
- A-3.04.00032: ITP Fire Fighting System (Status C)

### System Scope
Fire Fighting and Fire Alarm systems include:
- Fire suppression systems (sprinklers, foam systems, dry chemical)
- Fire detection and alarm networks
- Manual and automatic activation mechanisms
- Distribution piping and nozzles
- Control and monitoring equipment
- Associated electrical and mechanical components

## Shop Inspection Activities

Shop inspection activities are conducted at the manufacturer's facility prior to shipping to the project site.

### Material & Component Verification
- Supplier material certifications and Mill Test Reports (MTRs)
- Dimensional inspection of components against design specifications
- Visual inspection for manufacturing defects, corrosion, or damage
- Hydrostatic pressure testing of pressure vessels and piping systems
- Pressure relief valve setting and operation verification

### Assembly & Fabrication Testing
- Correct assembly of system components per design drawings
- Functionality testing of valve operations (manual and automatic)
- Fire alarm sensor calibration and sensitivity verification
- Control system functional testing
- Electrical continuity and insulation resistance testing

### Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)
- Complete system functional test under simulated design conditions
- Fire suppression activation testing (where applicable and safe)
- Alarm system end-to-end testing with all notification devices
- Documentation of all test results and any discrepancies noted

---

## Field/Site Installation Inspection Activities

Field inspection verifies proper installation and integration of fire fighting systems with project infrastructure.

### Pre-Installation Verification
- Receipt inspection of shipped components for damage
- Verification of component identification and traceability
- Storage conditions verification (temperature, humidity, protection)
- Confirmation of site preparation per specifications

### Installation Verification
- Correct routing and support of distribution piping
- Proper installation of sprinkler heads/nozzles per spacing requirements
- Fire alarm sensor placement verification per design
- Electrical connection and conduit installation per code
- Pressure test of installed piping systems before charging
- Relief valve setting verification on installed equipment

### System Charging & Pressurization
- Filling/pressurization of systems using approved media
- Pressure gauge installation and calibration verification
- Pressure regulator functionality testing
- Pilot line installation and leak testing
- System pressure stabilization and monitoring

### Integration & Commissioning
- Control system integration with Building Management System (BMS)
- Emergency shutdown circuit functionality
- Manual override testing
- Integration with fire alarm panel and notification devices
- Documentation update with actual installation configurations

---

## Functional & Commissioning Tests

Final system tests verify complete operational readiness under design conditions.

### Fire Suppression System Tests
- **Sprinkler System**: Head spray pattern verification, pressure regulation, flow rate measurement
- **Foam System**: Proportioner setting verification, foam quality and discharge pattern
- **CO2 System**: Discharge nozzle functionality, depressurization rate verification
- **Dry Chemical System**: Discharge mechanism actuation, powder flow verification

### Fire Alarm System Tests
- **Detector Sensitivity**: Calibration verification using approved test equipment
- **Notification Devices**: Audible and visual device operation verification
- **Panel Communication**: Transmission of alarm signals to remote monitoring
- **Backup Power**: UPS functionality and transfer switching
- **Emergency Voice System**: Clarity and intelligibility of announcements

### Integrated System Tests
- Simultaneous activation of suppression and alarm systems
- Zone-based operation verification
- Sequential timing verification
- Manual override and reset functionality
- Documentation of all test parameters and acceptance

## Hold Points & Witness Points

### Hold Points (H) — Must Be Approved Before Proceeding

**H1** - Shop Inspection & FAT approval by Owner's Representative/Consultant before shipment to site

**H2** - Pressure test results reviewed and approved prior to system charging

**H3** - Field installation pressure test completion with acceptance of results

**H4** - Fire suppression discharge test approval (if full system discharge test is conducted)

**H5** - Fire alarm activation and notification test approval before system handover

**H6** - Final commissioning test completion and documentation approval

**H7** - Training of Owner's operating staff completed and documented

### Witness Points (W) — Owner/Consultant Observation Required

**W1** - Factory functional testing of fire suppression components

**W2** - Field pressure test of installed piping systems

**W3** - Installation of control sensors and alarm notification devices

**W4** - Functional testing of manual activation switches and emergency circuits

**W5** - Integrated system operational test (fire suppression + alarm)

**W6** - System handover documentation review and final walk-through

## Acceptance Criteria & Test Parameters

### Pressure Testing
- **Test Pressure**: System design pressure + 50% minimum (or per design)
- **Duration**: Minimum 10 minutes at test pressure without visible leakage
- **Leakage Limit**: Zero visible leakage at joints and connections
- **Pressure Drop**: Not to exceed 0.5 bar over 30 minutes in static hold

### Fire Suppression Discharge
- **Sprinkler Head Spacing**: Per NFPA 13 or applicable standard (typically 3.7 m × 3.7 m for ordinary hazard)
- **Discharge Duration**: Minimum 30 minutes from water source at design pressure
- **Foam Solution Concentration**: Within ±1% of design specification
- **CO2 Discharge Rate**: Per manufacturer specification (typically 200-400 kg/min for large systems)

### Fire Alarm System
- **Detector Sensitivity**: Within manufacturer specifications (typically -30% to +20% of nominal)
- **Alarm Activation Time**: Detection to alarm signal within 30 seconds
- **Notification Device Sound Level**: Minimum 80 dB at 3 meters for audible devices
- **Visual Signal**: Steady or flashing per notification device type

### Electrical Testing
- **Insulation Resistance**: Minimum 1 MΩ at 500 VDC for control circuits
- **Continuity**: Zero resistance for electrical jumpers and connections
- **Voltage Drop**: Not exceeding 2% on continuous circuits
- **Circuit Breaker Trip Time**: Per manufacturer specification at rated current

## Critical Lessons & Best Practices

1. **Early Coordination**: Confirm FAT schedule with all parties 4-6 weeks in advance
2. **Pressure Test Safety**: Use appropriate pressure test equipment with relief valves; never exceed design pressure
3. **Documentation**: Photograph all test pressures, sensor locations, and final connections
4. **Nozzle Protection**: Protect sprinkler heads during construction to prevent clogging
5. **Alarm Integration**: Verify BMS communication before final handover
6. **Training Timing**: Conduct staff training immediately before system handover while installation is fresh in memory
7. **As-Built Drawings**: Update all drawings with actual installation locations and settings
