## 1. Scope & Objective

This document specifies the **Mandatory Spare Parts** that must be delivered and provided to the Owner (PT PLN) upon commissioning and start of commercial operation of the Bangkanai GEPP Stage 2 power plant.

### 1.1 Purpose of Mandatory Spare Parts
- **Initial Maintenance Buffer:** Critical components for the first 12–24 months of operation
- **Safety-Related:** Parts that if not available can cause safety shutdown (seals, bearings, gaskets)
- **High Wear Rate:** Components subject to normal wear (filter elements, seals, bearings)
- **Lead Time Protection:** Items with long delivery times (specialized rotors, nozzles)

### 1.2 Classification of Spare Parts

| Category | Definition | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Mandatory Spare Parts** | Contractually required spares included in contract price; delivered with equipment | Filter elements, seal kits, gaskets, bearing sets |
| **Recommended Spare Parts** | Suggested spare parts for optimized O&M; optional and priced separately | Additional filter element sets, extended seal kits |
| **Available Spare Parts** | Parts available on demand through Wärtsilä aftermarket supply network | Specialized rotors, rotor sets, power unit boards |

## 4. Critical Mandatory Spare Parts — Maintenance Interval Drivers

### 4.1 High-Frequency Replacement Items

These items are recommended for replacement at regular intervals and are included in the mandatory spare parts package:

#### 4.1.1 Filter Elements

**All Systems:** Filter elements (PAAE049335, PAAF001363, B00551, B00602, etc.)

| Component | Replacement Interval | Annual Consumption | Mandatory Qty Supplied |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Fuel Gas Filter Elements** | Every 2,000 hrs @ normal gas quality | ~4–5 per year | 1 |
| **Fuel Feeder Filter Cartridges** | Every 1,000 hrs @ 60–80°C operation | ~8–10 per year | 1 |
| **Strainer Filter (PAD Units)** | Every 500 hrs @ high-velocity flow | ~17–20 per year | 4 total |
| **Booster Unit Primary Filter (B00055)** | Every 1,500 hrs | ~6 per year | 1 per unit (16 total) |

**Critical Note:** Filter elements are wear items; plant cannot operate continuously without available spares. Mandatory package provides ~6–12 months supply.

#### 4.1.2 Seal Kits & Gaskets

**All Systems:** Seal kits (B00095, D00197, D00259, etc.), gaskets (B00186, V00164, etc.)

| Component | Failure Mode | Replacement Interval | Qty per Annual Maintenance |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Shaft Seal + O-Ring (D00195)** | Leakage when seal worn (wear groove in shaft) | Every 2,000–4,000 hrs | 1 per booster unit |
| **Gaskets (all types)** | Loss of seal when main equipment overhauled | Per major service cycle (every 12,000–24,000 hrs) | 56 (B00186 alone) |
| **O-Ring for Filter (QOC)** | Extrusion/hardening in high-temperature fuel gas | Every 2,000 hrs | Covered by mandatory 1 pc |

**Strategic Importance:** Seal failure can cause:
- Oil/fuel leakage → environmental contamination
- Loss of system pressure → equipment shutdown
- Safety hazard (hot oil spray)

**Mandatory package ensures** unplanned shutdown due to missing seals is prevented for 12–18 months.

### 4.2 Medium-Frequency Items (Partial Overhaul Components)

| Part Number | Description | Failure Mechanism | Typical Service Life |
|------------|-------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **D00212, M00001, M00006** | Ball Bearings | Fatigue wear; cage wear | 4,000–8,000 hours |
| **D00258** | Rotor Set (Impeller + Shaft) | Erosion if foreign particles in fuel; corrosion if water ingress | 10,000–20,000 hours |
| **Q00048, Q00049** | Valve Component Sets | Spring relaxation; orifice erosion | 8,000–16,000 hours |

**Mandatory Spares Provide:** ~1 complete rotor set per booster unit (cost EUR 1,830) enables quick replacement if rotor damage detected, avoiding 4–6 week factory rebuild lead time.

## 5. Spare Parts Delivery & Documentation Requirements

### 5.1 Delivery Schedule

**Contractual Requirement:** All mandatory spare parts must be delivered with main equipment to site (not shipped separately).

| Delivery Phase | Items Delivered | Condition | Document Required |
|----------------|-----------------|-----------|------------------|
| **With Main Equipment** | All filter elements, seal kits, gaskets, small bearings | Factory-sealed packaging; moisture-protected | Packing List + Spare Parts Certificate |
| **Pre-Commissioning (4–6 weeks before startup)** | Rotor sets, power unit boards, large assemblies | Factory-packaged; pressure-sealed if required | Certificate of Conformity + Inspection Report |
| **At COD (Commercial Operation Date)** | Full spare parts inventory transferred to Owner | Owner's warehouse; labeled & indexed | Spare Parts Handover Certificate |

### 5.2 Mandatory Documentation Accompanying Spares

**Each spare part shipment includes:**

1. **Packing List** — Item-by-item catalog with part numbers, quantities, unit price
2. **Certificate of Conformity (CE Mark)** — If EU-sourced (e.g., Wärtsilä parts)
3. **Material Test Certificates** — For pressure-boundary parts (gaskets, seals if ASME-stamped)
4. **Inspection & Test Reports** — For critical items (bearings, rotor assemblies)
5. **Spare Parts Storage Instructions** — Temperature, humidity, shelf-life limits
6. **Part Traceability** — Serial numbers for tracked components (rotor sets, motors)

### 5.3 Spare Parts Inventory Management (Post-Commissioning)

**Owner's Responsibility:**
- Maintain spare parts in dedicated warehouse, protected from moisture & contamination
- Track usage of consumables (filters, seals) in equipment logbook
- Replenish consumed items per Recommended Spare Parts schedule within 12 months
- Annual physical inventory audit per contract Schedule 2.3

**Contractor's Responsibility (Warranty Period):**
- Provide spare parts technical data sheets & replacement procedures
- Support Owner's technical staff with spare parts training (per Schedule 1.3 — Training)
- Replace defective spares discovered during warranty period (12 months from COD)

## 8. Spare Parts & Warranty Interaction

### 8.1 Warranty Period Spare Parts Coverage

**Defective Parts Replacement (0–12 months from COD):**
- If spare part found defective during installation or initial operation, Contractor replaces at no cost
- Contractor covers shipping & transportation back to site
- Replacement must occur within 14 days (expedited shipment)

**Example:** Seal kit (B00095) installed in booster unit during commissioning found defective at 100 hours operation → Contractor ships replacement overnight; original returned for analysis.

### 8.2 Post-Warranty Spare Parts Procurement

**After Warranty Period (12+ months from COD):**
- Owner procures additional recommended spares via Wärtsilä aftermarket sales network
- Prices per Schedule 2.3 (with annual escalation clause per CPI, capped at 3% per annum)
- Lead times increase (6–8 weeks vs. immediate availability during warranty)

## 9. Environmental & Safety Considerations

### 9.1 Hazardous Components in Spares

**MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheets) Provided:**
- **Lubricating Oil** (if rotor set includes bearings pre-lubricated) — Biodegradability statement; flash point > 200°C
- **Seal Material** (Fluorosilicone, PTFE) — Non-toxic; disposal per local environmental regulations

### 9.2 Disposal & End-of-Life

**Owner's Environmental Responsibility:**
- Used filter elements containing potential fuel residue → dispose per hazardous waste protocol
- Used seals & gaskets → standard municipal waste (non-hazardous)
- Used ball bearings (steel) → recyclable scrap metal
- Electronic control boards (Q00057) → electronic waste (WEEE directive, if EU) or equivalent

**Contractor Obligation:** Provide disposal guidelines in spare parts documentation.

## 10. Spare Parts Approval & Review Process

### 10.1 PLN/Owner Review of Spare Parts List

**Pre-Delivery Verification (Contractor to Provide):**

1. **Spare Parts Specification Document** — Complete list with part numbers, quantities, unit prices, lead times
2. **Maintenance Schedule Cross-Reference** — Showing which spares are consumed at which service interval
3. **Failure Mode Analysis (FMEA)** — Justifying inclusion of each mandatory spare based on failure rate & impact
4. **Cost Justification** — Demonstrating value of mandatory spares vs. recommended alternatives

**Owner's QC Review Process:**
- Technical review: Are quantities appropriate for Year 1–2 operation?
- Cost review: Are unit prices competitive? (benchmarked against industry)
- Lead time review: Are long-lead items included in mandatory package?
- Availability review: Are spares sourced from reliable suppliers with no obsolescence risk?

**Approval Gate:** Owner (PLN) approves spare parts list before Final Equipment Release (IRN) issued.

## 12. Conclusion & Strategic Value

### 12.1 Mandatory Spare Parts — Critical Success Factor

**Impact on Plant Availability:**
- **With Mandatory Spares:** 92%+ availability target achievable in Year 1–2 (per Performance Guarantee, Schedule 6)
- **Without Adequate Spares:** Availability drops to 70–80% due to unplanned shutdowns (filter blockage, seal failure, bearing wear)

**Financial Impact:**
- Mandatory spares cost: ~EUR 200k (0.5–1% of contract value)
- Unplanned outage penalty: ~EUR 50k–100k per day (lost revenue + liquidated damages)
- **ROI on spare parts package:** ~100:1 (2,000 euro investment prevents 200k–day outage)

### 12.2 Owner Action Items Post-Delivery

1. **Immediately:** Establish spare parts warehouse with environmental control (20–25°C, 45–60% RH)
2. **Within 1 Month:** Conduct staff training on spare parts handling, storage, requisition procedures
3. **Quarterly:** Monitor spare parts consumption vs. budgeted usage; reconcile with equipment running hours
4. **At Month 8 COD:** Review Year 2 spare parts needs; place orders for long-lead items (8–12 week lead time)
5. **At Month 12:** Handover to Operations & Maintenance team; finalize permanent storage location & security
