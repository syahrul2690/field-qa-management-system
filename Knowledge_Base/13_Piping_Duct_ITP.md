## Overview & Scope

Piping systems in power plants carry critical fluids (cooling water, steam, fuel oil, compressed air) at varying pressures and temperatures. Ductwork carries air for HVAC, smoke evacuation, and ventilation. This ITP establishes quality requirements for field installation, pressure testing, air-tightness verification, and functional performance validation.

**System Types Covered:**
- **Metallic Piping:** Carbon steel, stainless steel, copper (cooling, boiler feed, condensate)
- **Ductwork:** Spiral and rectangular sheet metal ducts for HVAC and industrial ventilation

**Applicable Standards:**
- **ASME B31.1:** Power Piping Code
- **ASME B16.5:** Pipe Flanges & Fittings
- **ASME Section VIII:** Pressure Vessel Code (for piping components)
- **SMACNA:** Sheet Metal and Air Conditioning Contractors Manual (Ductwork)
- **ASTM A106:** Seamless Carbon Steel Pipe (power generation)
- **ASTM A53:** Welded and Seamless Carbon Steel Pipe

### Pressure Testing (Field Installation)

| Phase | Test Pressure | Duration | Acceptance | Hold Point |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **System Pressure Test** | **1.5× Design Pressure** | **10 minutes minimum** | **Zero leakage** | **H** |
| **Safety Valve Proof Test** | **110% of set pressure** | As per valve spec | Proper opening, full flow | H |
| **Final Acceptance Test** | **Design Pressure** | 30 minutes minimum | Stable pressure, no loss | H |

**Test Procedure:**
1. Fill system slowly, bleed air from high points
2. Pressurize slowly to 25%, 50%, 75%, then 100%
3. Hold at test pressure for required duration
4. Monitor pressure gauge (calibrated, ±2% accuracy)
5. Inspect all joints, welds, connections for leakage
6. Document pressure vs. time graph
7. Signature by QA engineer and witness

## Functional & Commissioning Tests

| Activity | Acceptance | Duration |
|----------|---|---|
| **Flow Velocity Check** | ≥10 ft/s through main lines | Until achieved |
