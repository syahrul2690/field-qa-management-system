## Overview & Scope

This knowledge base consolidates critical Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) requirements for civil works, steel structure works, and building works in power plant construction projects. The ITPs document establish comprehensive quality control frameworks, mandatory inspection activities, acceptance criteria, and verification procedures aligned with international standards (AWS, ASTM, DIN, ISO) and project specifications.

### Purpose
The Inspection and Test Plan forms an integral part of control/verification documentation. It defines inspection activities, hold points, witness points, and acceptance criteria to ensure construction quality and compliance with contract requirements and technical specifications.

### Project Scope Coverage
- **Civil Works:** Foundations, excavations, concrete works, paving, soil works
- **Steel Structure Works:** Fabrication, erection, welding, connections
- **Building Works:** Concrete structures, reinforcement, formwork, finishing

### Key Inspection Categories for Steel Works

#### Material Verification
- **Material Certification**
  - Mill certificates for all steel grades
  - Chemical composition verification
  - Mechanical properties (yield strength, tensile strength, elongation)
  - Standards: ASTM A36, ASTM A572, DIN 17100

- **Dimensional Verification**
  - Checking of dimensions, flatness, surface condition
  - Inspection Code: **SW** (Spot Witness)

- **Storage and Handling**
  - Protection from weather and corrosion
  - Proper stacking to prevent deformation
  - Inspection Code: **W** (Witness Point)

#### Fabrication Activities

- **Cutting Operations**
  - Cutting method (thermal or mechanical)
  - Dimensional accuracy
  - Edge quality
  - Inspection Code: **SW** (Spot Witness)

- **Drilling and Punching**
  - Hole size and location tolerance
  - Surface condition around holes
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **SW** (Spot Witness)

- **Welding Operations**
  - Weld Procedure Specification (WPS) approval
  - Welder qualification records (WQTR)
  - Workmanship inspection
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **W** (Witness Point)

#### Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

- **Visual Inspection (VT)**
  - Surface cracks, porosity, undercut
  - Dimensional acceptance
  - Inspection Code: **W** (Witness Point)

- **Radiography (RT)**
  - Internal defects detection
  - Penetrability and contrast
  - Standards: ASTM E94, EN 12517
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **R** (Review)

- **Ultrasonic Testing (UT)**
  - Flaw detection and sizing
  - Thickness measurement
  - Standards: ASTM E494, EN 12668
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **R** (Review)

- **Penetrant Testing (PT)**
  - Surface-breaking defects
  - Standards: ASTM E165, EN 12763
  - Inspection Code: **R** (Review)

- **Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)**
  - Ferrous material surface and near-surface defects
  - Standards: ASTM E1316, EN 12699
  - Inspection Code: **R** (Review)

#### Bolted Connections

- **Bolt Quality**
  - Grade compliance (Grade 8.8, 10.9, etc.)
  - Mill certificates
  - Surface condition
  - Inspection Code: **SW** (Spot Witness)

- **Bolt Installation**
  - Proper tensioning (turn-of-nut method, calibrated wrench)
  - Thread engagement
  - Hole size tolerance
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **SW** (Spot Witness)

- **Bolt Inspection**
  - Ultrasonic bolt tension verification
  - Visual inspection for proper installation
  - Acceptance: Inspection reports
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point)

#### Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)

- **PWHT Procedure Verification**
  - Temperature monitoring and recording
  - Holding time compliance
  - Heating and cooling rates
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **R** (Review)

- **Hardness Testing** (if required)
  - Heat-affected zone testing
  - Standards: ASTM E18, EN 6507
  - Acceptance: Test reports
  - Inspection Code: **R** (Review)

#### Surface Preparation and Coating

- **Surface Cleaning**
  - Removal of mill scale, rust, paint
  - Standard: ASTM D6386 (SA-2.5, Near-white blast cleaning)
  - Acceptance: Visual comparison with standards
  - Inspection Code: **W** (Witness Point)

- **Coating Application**
  - Paint system compliance
  - Surface profile (anchor pattern)
  - Dry film thickness (DFT) measurement
  - Standards: NACE/SSPC paint systems
  - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point), **SW** (Spot Witness)

   - Activity: Formwork must be inspected and approved before concrete placement

3. **Reinforcement Placement**

4. **Concrete Placement Readiness**
   - Activity: All preparations checked before concrete pour
   - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point)
   - Checks: Form cleanliness, support adequacy, rebar placement, temperature
   - Acceptance: Conditions ready for placement
   - Verifying Document: Pre-placement checklist

   - Activity: Curing method and schedule approved
   - Inspection Code: **H** (Hold Point)
   - Acceptance: Curing method (wet burlap, curing compound, etc.) and duration (28 days minimum)

6. **Formwork Removal**
   - Activity: Approval before removal to ensure concrete has achieved adequate strength
   - Inspection Code: **W** (Witness Point)

## Quality Control Checkpoints & Hold Points

### Critical Hold Points (H) — Work Cannot Proceed Without Acceptance

#### Civil Works Domain

1. **Soil Investigation & Field Exploration (2.0)**
   - Reference: HD No. 5-101
   - Required: Soil stratification report, geotechnical assessment
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

2. **Survey Reference Points (3.0-3.4)**
   - Reference: GEPP-BKN-C-DW-001
   - Required: Benchmark coordinates, elevation data
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer
   - Tolerance: Horizontal ±50 mm, Vertical ±30 mm (typical)

3. **Reinforcement Rebar Work (7.0)**
   - Reference: GEPP-BKN-C-CS-002
   - Required: Material mill certificates, placement inspection report
   - Checks: Grade compliance, surface condition, spacing accuracy
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

4. **Concrete Placement Preparation (8.0 pre-pour)**
   - Required: Mix design approval, slump test, air content
   - Checks: Formwork readiness, rebar position verification, cleanliness
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

5. **Concrete Mix Design (8.0)**
   - Standard: ASTM C94, ACI 309
   - Required: w/c ratio, aggregate grading, admixture selection
   - Acceptance: Slump 100-150 mm (typical), air content 4-6%
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Consultant/Engineer

6. **Concrete Compressive Strength (8.17)**
   - Standard: ASTM C39 (compression test)
   - Schedule: 3-day, 7-day, 28-day tests
   - Acceptance: Minimum 28-day strength per design (typically 350-400 kg/cm²)
   - Cylinders taken: 1 per 50-100 m³ concrete
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

7. **Piling Works (12.0)**
   - Reference: LD No. 5-320, GEPP-BKN-C-CS-003
   - Required: PDA test, preliminary load test per ASTM D4945-1996
   - Acceptance: Verified bearing capacity, acceptable blow count
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

#### Steel Structure Domain

1. **Weld Procedure Specification (WPS) Approval**
   - Required: AWS D1.1 compliance, PQR (Procedure Qualification Record)
   - Checks: Heat input, cooling rate, PWHT if required
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

2. **Radiography (RT) Results**
   - Standard: ASTM E94, EN 12517
   - Required: No critical defects (cracks, lack of fusion, porosity > 2 mm)
   - Acceptance: Accept per ASTM E505 or equivalent

3. **Bolt Tensioning Verification**
   - Standard: ASTM A490, ASTM A325
   - Method: Ultrasonic bolt tension measurement or calibrated wrench
   - Acceptance: Within specified tension range (±10% typical)

4. **Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
   - Standard: ASTM E494
   - Monitoring: Temperature recording device (chart recorder)
   - Acceptance: Minimum temperature per WPS, heating/cooling rates within limits

#### Building Works Domain

1. **Formwork Design Approval**
   - Required: Structural calculations, design drawings
   - Factor of Safety: Minimum 2.0 for dead load + construction load
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Structural Engineer

2. **Concrete Mix Design (8.0)**
   - Required: Mix design report per ACI 211
   - Slump specification per concrete placement method
   - Air content verification (ASTM C173 or ASTM C231)

3. **Reinforcement Placement Check (8.6)**
   - Reference: Approved drawing
   - Checks: Bar location ±20 mm, cover depth ±10 mm, lap splice length verification
   - Approval Authority: Contractor + Owner/Engineer

4. **Concrete Placement Approval (8.12)**
   - Reference: ASTM C94
   - Required: Ambient temperature between 5°C-40°C (typical)
   - Checks: Formwork stability, weather protection plan
   - Workmanship: Proper vibration, drop height ≤ 1.5 m

5. **Concrete Strength Verification (8.17)**
   - Standard: ASTM C39, ACI 318
   - Acceptance: 28-day minimum design strength
   - Retest: If strength < 90% design, investigate and retest additional cylinders

6. **Curing & Early Strength Development (8.14)**
   - Method: Wet curing minimum 7 days, preferably 28 days
   - Monitoring: Daily moisture/temperature checks
   - Formwork removal: Not before 70% of 28-day design strength

## Technical Glossary

| Term | Definition | Reference |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Hold Point (H)** | A notification point where work may not proceed beyond this point without acceptance or formal waiver from the Inspection Authority (Client/Owner). Critical approval gate. | ITP Legend |
| **Witness Point (W)** | Inspection activity requiring presence of Client/Owner and Sub-Contractor. Work may proceed if Client/Owner does not appear at the scheduled time, but notification must be provided. | ITP Legend |
| **Spot Witness (SW)** | Inspection activity performed at spot or random sampling locations (random sample basis). Client/Owner witness presence required during inspection. | ITP Legend |
| **Review (R)** | Review of verifying certificate, documentation, inspection report, or process to ensure compliance with specification/requirements. Requires examination and approval. | ITP Legend |
| **Approval (A)** | Formal approval of documentation (Procedure, Work Instruction, Inspection Report, Control Document). Must be signed and dated by authorized personnel. | ITP Legend |
| **Information (IR)** | Document or report issued for information purposes only (e.g., Inspection Report, Mill Certificate, Fabrication Completion Certificate). | ITP Legend |
| **Inspection and Test Plan (ITP)** | A comprehensive document defining inspection activities, hold points, witness points, acceptance criteria, and verification procedures for quality assurance during construction. Forms an integral part of control/verification documentation. | Contract Documents |
| **Mill Certificate** | Documentation provided by material supplier confirming grade, composition, mechanical properties (yield, tensile strength, elongation), and chemical analysis of material. | Material Verification |
| **Compressive Strength (Concrete)** | Ability of hardened concrete to resist compressive loads, tested using ASTM C39 at 3, 7, and 28 days. Measured in MPa or kg/cm². | Concrete Testing |
| **w/c Ratio (Water/Cement Ratio)** | Ratio of water to cement weight in concrete mix. Controls concrete strength and durability. Typical range 0.40-0.60. | Mix Design |
| **Slump** | Measure of concrete workability; vertical distance (mm) concrete slumps when test cone is removed. Typical range 50-150 mm. | Concrete Properties |
| **Lap Splice** | Overlapping length of two reinforcing bars to develop tension capacity. Minimum length per code = 40-50 × bar diameter. | Rebar Placement |
| **Formwork** | Temporary mold/support structure into which concrete is cast. Must be designed for dead load + construction loads with 2.0 safety factor minimum. | Concrete Construction |
| **Curing** | Process of maintaining proper moisture and temperature in freshly placed concrete to develop strength. Minimum 7 days wet curing (preferably 28 days). | Concrete Finishing |
| **Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)** | Controlled heating and cooling cycle applied to welded components to relieve residual stress and improve metallurgical properties. Typical temperature: 600-650°C for low-alloy steel. | Steel Welding |
| **Weld Procedure Specification (WPS)** | Document detailing welding parameters (electrode, amperage, voltage, travel speed, heat input, cooling rate) required for quality welds. Must be qualified by Procedure Qualification Record (PQR). | Welding Quality |
| **Welder Qualification Test Record (WQTR)** | Documentation of welder competency test results demonstrating ability to produce code-compliant welds per applicable welding standard (AWS D1.1). | Welding Personnel |
| **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)** | Inspection methods that do not damage the component being tested. Includes: Visual (VT), Radiography (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Penetrant (PT), Magnetic Particle (MT). | Quality Control |
| **Radiography (RT)** | Non-destructive test using X-rays or gamma rays to detect internal defects (cracks, voids, lack of fusion) in welds and structures. Per ASTM E94, EN 12517. | Steel Inspection |
| **Ultrasonic Testing (UT)** | Non-destructive test using high-frequency sound waves to detect flaws and measure thickness. Per ASTM E494, EN 12668. | Steel Inspection |
| **Penetrant Testing (PT)** | Non-destructive test using liquid dye to detect surface-breaking defects. Per ASTM E165, EN 12763. | Steel Inspection |
| **Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)** | Non-destructive test for ferrous materials using magnetic field to detect surface and subsurface defects. Per ASTM E1316, EN 12699. | Steel Inspection |
| **Bolt Tension Verification** | Inspection method using ultrasonic tools or calibrated wrenches to verify bolts are installed at proper tension (within ±10% of specified). | Bolted Connections |
| **Concrete Placement Method** | Technique for depositing concrete into formwork, including drop height (≤ 1.5 m), vibration method (internal or external), consolidation control. | Concrete Workmanship |
| **Cover (Concrete)** | Thickness of concrete protecting reinforcing steel from exposure. Typical values: 40-50 mm for interior, 50-75 mm for exposed surfaces. | Rebar Protection |
| **Blast Cleaning (Surface Prep)** | Surface preparation removing mill scale, rust, and coating to bare steel per ASTM D6386 (Near-white blast cleaning = SA-2.5). | Steel Surface Prep |
| **Dry Film Thickness (DFT)** | Measurement of dried coating thickness on steel surface. Verified using electromagnetic DFT gauge per NACE/SSPC standards. | Coating Inspection |
| **PDA Test** | Pile Driving Analyzer test measuring dynamic bearing capacity during pile installation using strain and acceleration data. Per ASTM D4945-1996. | Piling Quality |
| **Load Test (Preliminary)** | Static or dynamic load test verifying pile bearing capacity prior to full-scale construction. Per ASTM D4945-1996. | Piling Quality |
| **Blow Count** | Number of hammer blows required to drive a pile a specified distance (typically 30 cm). Indicator of bearing capacity and soil resistance. | Piling Installation |
| **As-Built Drawing** | Marked-up drawing showing actual construction as completed, including all changes, deviations, and field modifications from original design. | Final Documentation |
| **Deviation Report** | Documentation of any departure from contract specification or technical requirement, including justification and approval. | Quality Records |
| **Non-Conformance Report (NCR)** | Formal documentation of quality non-conformance, including impact assessment, corrective action, and owner approval for acceptance or rework. | Quality Management |

## Quality Assurance Framework Summary

### Approval Chain
**SubContractor → Contractor (PP) → Consultant/Engineer → Owner (PLN)**

All Hold Points require formal written approval with dated signatures before work proceeds.

### Documentation Requirements
Each activity must be supported by:
- Inspection report with signature and date
- Test certificates (if applicable)
- Photographic evidence (for critical items)
- Reference to approved drawings
- Acceptance or rejection determination

### Non-Conformance Management
If acceptance criteria are not met:
1. Issue Non-Conformance Report (NCR)
2. Contractor proposes corrective action
3. Owner review and approval required
4. Implementation and verification
5. Formal acceptance or rework decision

### Revision Control
All ITPs maintain revision history documenting changes, approval dates, and status progression (A, B, C, etc.)
