**Status:** Status B (Approved as Noted)

---

## Overview & Scope

This ITP encompasses comprehensive field installation and functional commissioning activities for all mechanical and electrical systems in the power plant facility. It establishes a unified quality assurance framework covering:

- **Mechanical Systems:** Rotating equipment, piping, ductwork, tanks, cranes, HVAC
- **Electrical Systems:** Power distribution, switchgear, transformers, motors, controls
- **Integrated Testing:** System-level performance verification, load testing, efficiency validation

**Critical Objective:** Ensure all equipment and systems are installed per design specifications, tested for functionality, and accept operational responsibility with documented evidence of compliance.

**Document Structure:**
- **Part 1:** Field Installation Inspection (equipment delivery through pre-commissioning)
- **Part 2:** Functional Testing (system startup, performance validation, handover)
